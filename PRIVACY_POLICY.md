# Privacy Policy for Xtractor

Last Updated: November 2026

**Xtractor** ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Extension.

### 1. Zero Data Collection
Xtractor **does not collect, transmit, store, or sell** any personally identifiable information, browsing history, cookies, credentials, or personal data.

### 2. Local Processing
All functions performed by Xtractor—including:
- Inspecting network requests for video and audio MIME types
- Parsing HLS (.m3u8) playlists
- Downloading and decrypting video stream chunks
- Merging segments into video files
operate **strictly locally** on your device inside your browser sandbox. No telemetry, analytics, or browsing information is ever transmitted to external servers.

### 3. Permissions Used
- **webRequest & <all_urls>**: Used solely to passively observe media header types (e.g., `video/mp4`, `application/vnd.apple.mpegurl`) on pages you visit so you can download them.
- **downloads**: Used solely to save the downloaded media files to your computer's Downloads directory.
- **storage**: Used solely to persist your local user preferences (e.g. minimum file size threshold).

### 4. Third-Party Services
Xtractor does not integrate with any third-party analytics, trackers, or advertising SDKs.

### 5. Changes to This Policy
Any changes to this policy will be reflected in this document.

### Contact
If you have any questions, please open an issue in the project repository.
